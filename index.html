Wärtsilä ESG & Circular Economy Prototype

AI-assisted sustainability overview

Overview

Wärtsilä is a Finnish technology company focusing on sustainable solutions for marine and energy industries. This prototype demonstrates how AI can be used to summarise ESG information from sustainability reports.

ESG Summary

Environmental

Wärtsilä focuses on decarbonisation, energy efficiency and reducing emissions through advanced technologies and lifecycle optimisation.

Social

The company promotes employee wellbeing, safety, human rights and responsible supply chain practices globally.

Governance

Wärtsilä follows strong governance practices including ESG reporting, ethical business conduct and risk management.

Circular Economy

Wärtsilä supports circular economy through lifecycle services, maintenance, retrofitting and optimisation of existing systems. This reduces waste and extends the lifetime of products.

AI Insight

AI can analyse large ESG reports, extract key metrics and present them in simplified dashboards. This improves decision-making and makes sustainability information easier to understand.
