Wärtsilä Circular Economy & Sustainability Dashboard

A prototype website that summarises Wärtsilä’s sustainability information into clear ESG themes, circular economy insights and visual dashboard indicators.

Project Overview

Wärtsilä is a Finnish technology company operating in the marine and energy sectors. This prototype demonstrates how AI can support ESG reporting by simplifying long sustainability reports into understandable summaries and dashboard views.

ESG Summary

🌱

Environmental

Decarbonisation, energy efficiency, lifecycle optimisation and solutions that support the transition towards lower-emission marine and energy systems.

👥

Social

Employee wellbeing, occupational safety, human rights and responsible supply chain practices across Wärtsilä’s global operations.

⚖️

Governance

Ethical business conduct, transparent ESG reporting, risk management and sustainability-related decision-making.

Circular Economy

Wärtsilä supports circular economy through maintenance, modernisation, retrofitting and lifecycle services. These actions extend the lifetime of existing systems and reduce the need for unnecessary replacement.

Key idea

Instead of only producing new equipment, circular economy focuses on keeping existing assets useful for longer.

🤖 How AI Was Used

AI was used to summarise Wärtsilä’s sustainability information into clear ESG categories and circular economy themes. In a real system, AI could automatically read annual ESG reports, extract key indicators, detect trends and update this dashboard with new data.
